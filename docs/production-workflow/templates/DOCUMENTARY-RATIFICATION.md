Do not use HEAD alone to identify a modified or untracked file. The algorithm is
mandatorily `SHA-256` over the exact bytes stored on disk, without end-of-line
normalization, recording `LF` or `CRLF` to avoid divergence caused by
`core.autocrlf` on Windows. Pre-repository exploration (node A) is free and does
not require this receipt; the receipt begins when a candidate document starts to
be drafted and submitted to commitment. This receipt stays outside the bytes
whose digest it records. Do not fill in a fictitious hash.

A hash proves identity of bytes, not consent. If the executor can edit the sole
proof of acceptance, do not claim independent control. A receipt bound to a
`run_id` and `step_id` cannot be reused in another run or step. A check completed
before a pause does not waive re-checking the content immediately after the gate
and before using it.
